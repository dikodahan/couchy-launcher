Updated open-source fork of conreo/Couchy Launcher.
• Optional weather and seven-day forecasts.
• App renaming, custom banners and icon-pack support.
• Lower-memory rendering and bounded aerial video caching.
• Compatible Telegram/ADB/local backups with safer imports and export fallback.
• Built-in Home switching through Android or approved local ADB, with stock-launcher recovery.
• Android 9+ minimum; updated Telegram/build dependencies and normal HTTPS validation.
Thanks to conreo, Xaero252 and 9Chrk for the original work and contributions.
